# VikashSinha.dev Blog Cover Design System v1

Status: Approved working design direction, 11 October 2026. Every individual cover must be reviewed before publication.

## Purpose
Express a personal insight, decision dilemma or learned principle visually. Use a thoughtful, modern editorial style distinct from MBM's commercially diagnostic brand.

## Topics
Personal thought leadership, Inside the Buying Committee newsletter articles, B2B buyer/leadership insights, IIMA AGMP learning when accurately sourced, professional observations, and product-building experiences.

## Identity and composition
- Follow the currently implemented VikashSinha.dev website visual identity; do not force MBM's brand palette onto the personal site.
- Use restrained typography, thoughtful symbolic/architectural imagery and one conceptual idea per cover.
- Prefer minimalist, intellectually engaging composition over crowded marketing graphics and generic stock imagery.
- Supply a 1200 x 630 px master for social sharing and a separately checked 16:9 website crop as needed.
- Keep important text inside mobile-safe regions; provide specific accessible alt text.
- Prefer insight-led editorial titles, not clickbait.
- Do not fabricate stories, quotations, results, credentials or academic attributions.

## Publication workflow
1. Confirm an original article and audience intent.
2. Propose a relevant illustration, headline approach and composition.
3. Review for identity, originality, text legibility and editorial appropriateness.
4. Obtain Vikash's approval for each cover before use.
5. Track approved asset version and link to the published website article and LinkedIn newsletter edition, if applicable.

## Cross-channel rule
Share research and core thinking with Inside the Buying Committee, but treat each publication's formatting, URLs and SEO considerations deliberately. MBM covers remain visually distinct.
